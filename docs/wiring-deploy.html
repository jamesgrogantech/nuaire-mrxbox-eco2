MRXBOX HA Deploy Wiring

Permanent install: powered from the unit's own 12 V via a buck converter, reading the bus at 1200 baud over WiFi to Home Assistant. No laptop, no USB.

Set the buck output to 5.0 V with a meter BEFORE wiring it to the Pico. Trim-pot bucks ship at random voltages; VSYS tolerates max 5.5 V, and 12 V straight in destroys the Pico.

Bring-up order

  • Set the buck to 5.0 V on the bench (12V or any source in, meter on the output, turn the trim-pot) before anything connects to the Pico.
  • Wire grounds first, then A/B to the MAX485, then RO/3V3, then last the buck 5 V to VSYS.
  • Power the unit; the Pico boots, joins WiFi, reads the bus. No USB needed — but you can still plug USB for firmware updates (that's why the Schottky matters if both power sources meet).
  • If the MAX485 module has a 120 Ω terminator and reception looks noisy on an already-terminated bus, lift it.

Read-only by design — DE+RE grounded means the Pico physically cannot drive the bus. Firmware reads 1200 8N1 on GP5, bit-reverses data bytes, and publishes humidity / temperature / fan state to Home Assistant. Companion to the bench diagram in docs/wiring.html and the field map in docs/protocol.md.
