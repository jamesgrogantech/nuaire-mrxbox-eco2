MRXBOX Sniffer Wiring

Pico W + RS485 module tapped onto the MVHR display connector for bus sniffing. Five wires now, one dashed option for later.

Two rules before anything touches the unit: run the RS485 module at 3.3 V (RP2040 pins are not 5 V-tolerant), and identify all four MVHR pins with the meter before connecting a single wire — the 12 V pin stays unconnected in this phase.

Identifying the four MVHR pins

PinMeter signature (DC to GND)
GND0 V, rock steady. Find first: probe pin pairs until one reads a steady 12 V — the lower pin of that pair is GND
12 VSteady ~12 V, no flicker
AIdle ~1.5–3.3 V, the higher of the two data lines; flickers with traffic
BIdle lower than A (often 0.5–1.5 V); flickers with traffic
  • Getting GND right matters most — tying the unit's 12 V rail to Pico ground shorts it through USB and can damage the unit's PSU.
  • Both data lines steady at ~0 V usually means the bus is just quiet — wait 30 s+, sensors poll infrequently.
  • Cheap modules often carry a 120 Ω termination resistor. If reception looks corrupted on a bus that already has termination, remove it. Keep the tap stub under ~30 cm.
